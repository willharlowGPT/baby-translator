How the sounds are interpreted

Neh — hunger
Owh — tired / sleepy
Eh — needs burping
Eairh — lower wind / tummy discomfort
Heh — general discomfort

Baby Translator is an experimental aid, not a medical device or diagnosis. If your baby seems unwell, use normal medical advice regardless of the result.
